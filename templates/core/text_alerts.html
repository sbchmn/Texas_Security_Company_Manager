Why the number, not the person

Carriers reissue phone numbers. If consent followed you instead of the number, a phone you left behind could keep getting your schedule — and the person who has it now would be getting texts they never agreed to. So changing your number below is not switching your opt-in across: it stops the texts until the new number is opted in.

Required notices — a closed post, a timecard exception, a licence about to lapse — are categorised apart from anything optional. By email those still go out even if you unsubscribed from routine mail. By text they do not: an opted-out number gets nothing, because the rule about texts is about the number, not about how important we think our message is.
