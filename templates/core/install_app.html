Prepare the offline clock

  1. Sign in while connected and open Time clock.
  2. Wait for the secure clock to start before leaving coverage. The saved page contains your last-loaded posts, not live scheduling.
  3. Reconnect and open Time clock within 12 hours to synchronize punches. Keep the app open until it confirms synchronization.

Only the time clock supports offline capture. Payroll, records, schedule changes and sign-in need connectivity. Location is requested when recording a punch; camera permission is requested only for an applicable selfie workflow. No background tracking or background-sync guarantee is provided.

Use offline clocking only on your personal device, not a shared phone or kiosk. Sign-out and company switching remove the saved clock page, but do not erase queued punches. Synchronize before signing out, changing company, clearing browser data or uninstalling. Clearing app storage can permanently lose unsynchronized punches.

Updates and permissions

Web changes arrive from the server. Offline assets update after the browser activates a new worker; close all app windows and reopen after syncing. If installation or offline preparation fails, stay online and report the visible error to your administrator.

If location or camera is denied, review this site's browser/app permissions. Missing evidence may send a punch to review; installation does not bypass payroll, geofence or MFA rules.
