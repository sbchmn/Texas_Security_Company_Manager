Recurring posts

A series states one tour — a post, a window, and the days it stands — and produces ordinary shifts for any range of dates you generate. Each generated post then belongs to the schedule, not to the series: it can be edited, filled, or cancelled on its own, and it resolves its own pay and bill rate when the week is read.

How a series differs from a roster

Editing a series does not rewrite the posts it already produced — it changes what the next generation makes. Pausing stops further generation and keeps every post. Removing the series keeps the posts too, and they stop being traceable to a pattern. Generation refuses a date the post cannot be stood for — an officer who does not qualify, a site already covered in that hour, or a date this series already made — and the preview names the reason for each one rather than leaving a hole to find later.
