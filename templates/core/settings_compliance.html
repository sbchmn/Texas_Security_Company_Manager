Record types

Audience decides who a record is issued to: filed against a person's file, handed to every worker, or held for administrators only. Secrecy is a separate choice, because a workers' compensation claim and a payroll receipt are both attached to one person — Restricted takes the record out of the general personnel file and leaves it with the roles listed here, and Sealed additionally keeps it from the officer it is about.
