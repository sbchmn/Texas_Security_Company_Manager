Leave bank policy

Optional annual hours bank. HR enrolls each eligible employee on their personnel Profile.

How grants, approvals and payroll settlement work

Bank-year grant, accrual, carryover and pay-calendar terms are fixed when the year is first recorded. Changes apply to new enrollments and future years, not hours already earned. Use audited HR adjustments for current-year changes.

Annual upfront grants are prorated from eligibility through December 31. Per-period accrual earns eligible calendar days only after each completed period, with the final year fragment settled at January 1. Zero carryover expires unused available hours; reserved hours remain committed.

The daily default counts every local calendar day touched, excluding an end exactly at midnight. It is a suggestion, never automatic approval. Confirmed hours are reserved at approval and cannot exceed available hours. Disabling the bank blocks new enrollments and bank approvals; existing bank commitments and year terms are retained.

Worked hours never receive leave pay as well. Unused reserved hours return to the bank at whole-period payroll approval. Reopening restores the reservation and reverses that refund; if the refund has already been spent, HR must restore sufficient hours first. Bank leave is settled by whole-period approval, not a partial company-wide lock.

Configure Approved leave as paid before approving bank leave. Paidness is separate from approving an absence.
