Two rules worth knowing

Nobody else can have your PIN. Two officers on one code makes a punch at a shared station unattributable, so the company refuses a PIN that is already taken, and a supervisor who tells you a code is only doing it until you change it here.

Four digits is the shortest code allowed and the weakest. Anything a visitor could reach should be six or eight.
