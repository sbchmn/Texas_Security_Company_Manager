Archived — recoverable

An archive disposition keeps every byte and takes the record out of the live file, the compliance queue and the reminders. Restoring one is a decision reversal, so it asks for a reason and names you against it; the record stays downloadable in the meantime, because retention is not destruction.

Deleted — not recoverable

A permanent deletion removes the stored file, so nothing on this screen can bring the document back. The row stays as a tombstone with its hash, its type and its holder, which is what lets the company evidence that a record existed, was disposed of under a second approver, and is now absent by decision rather than by loss.
