Defaults record a reading, not a rule. The FLSA excludes training from hours worked only when it is outside normal hours, voluntary, not job-related and produces no work, so a required pre-assignment briefing is paid time; a meal period is unpaid only when the officer is completely relieved of duty, so a desk-side meal is working time; travel between properties during a shift is hours worked while the drive from home to the first post is not; and no wage law requires a holiday or weekend premium at all, which is why those multiples start at 1.00 and move by contract.
