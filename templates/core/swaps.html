Shift moves

Two kinds of move need a decision here: a post one officer is handing to a colleague, and a trade of two posts between two officers. A trade is approved as one pair — approving only one half would release an officer from a post while nobody took the other. Every eligibility check below was re-run now, at the moment coverage actually changes hands.
