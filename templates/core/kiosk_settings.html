PINs

Every officer sets their own from My clock PIN. If someone cannot — no sign-in yet, a phone that will not hold a browser — issue one from their personnel profile and hand it over the way you would hand over a key. It is never shown again after it is saved, and it is never stored in a form anyone can read back.

A station pauses after twenty refused PINs in five minutes. That is the bound a four-digit code needs on a device anyone can walk up to; use six or eight digits for a station a visitor could reach, and clear the pause from the list above when it is only a jammed button.
